# Review Orchestration

Match review depth to the change and request. Small, low-risk changes may need no separate reviewer; use an independent reviewer for meaningful behavioral risk, complex changes, or when requested. Give the reviewer the scope, expected behavior, and relevant constraints without prescribing conclusions.

Parallel independent reviews can help for distinct risks or complex changes when delegation is available and authorized. Consolidate by evidence, not vote count. Investigate actionable findings, make fixes in scope, and recheck affected behavior. Report unresolved findings and verification gaps.
