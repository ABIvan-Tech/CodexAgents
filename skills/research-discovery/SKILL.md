# Research Discovery

Start broad with file patterns, ownership, and entry points; narrow to execution paths, contracts, tests, and relevant documentation. Follow applicable project guidance before investigation.

Scale depth to the question: quick for a known location, broader for multiple interacting surfaces, and thorough when risk or uncertainty requires it. Stop when the question is answered or report what remains unknown. Return precise paths and symbols, evidence, relevant constraints, and open questions. Do not treat assumptions as facts.
